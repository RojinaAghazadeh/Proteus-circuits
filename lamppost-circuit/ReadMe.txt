A lamppost circuit is a simple automatic lighting system that turns a lamp ON when it is dark and OFF when there is sufficient light. In Proteus, this circuit can be implemented using an LDR (Light Dependent Resistor), a transistor, a resistor, and an LED or lamp.

The LDR changes its resistance according to the amount of light falling on it. During daylight, the LDR has low resistance, keeping the lamp OFF. When the surroundings become dark, the resistance of the LDR increases, causing the transistor to switch ON and power the lamp.

This project demonstrates the practical use of sensors and switching devices in automatic control systems. It is commonly used as a basic model for street lights and outdoor lighting systems that operate without manual control.
